# Queen Sofonisba

## Faction: Royal Delisgar

Sprawling Delisgar has dominated the Copper Coast since time immemorial. Once, every knee for two hundred miles bent to the king in Brialgon. Time and decadence have withered the flower of empire, and the word of the throne is not what it once was. Infidel armies press in from the east, and even the Prince of Allakia no longer obeys without question. Nevertheless, the Delisgrene will is not yet broken, and where the imperial armies march, the earth shakes.

With the shocking fall of the Diamond Isle to the Sultanate of Dinn, the realm clamors for war. Many holy relics of the Thousand Saints rest in the monasteries of the Isle, and it is unthinkable for infidels to defile them.

## Identity

What frustrates a great leader more than to reap the consequences of others’ failures? For twenty years you have held the reins of this realm, and still it bucks and chafes at the bit. Your father was weak, as was his mother, and all your ancestors back to foolish Pophanes who failed to exterminate the Allakian barbarians when he had the chance. 
You will make no such mistakes. The decline of Delisgar ends with you. By the Thousand Saints have you sworn it; in the shrines and in the Hagiomphalos have you sworn it: your sword will drink blood until the last knee bends or breaks.

- Age: 41
- Faith: Hagiogue
- Epithet: The Black

## Goals

*As determined by yourself and the Senate.*

1.	Reconquer the Diamond Isle.
2.	Reclaim the port at Orbost.
3.	Bring your cousin Prince Giubrin to heel.
