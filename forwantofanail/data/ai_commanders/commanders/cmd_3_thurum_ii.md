# Sultan Thurum II

## Faction: Sultanate of Dinn

The past century has seen Dinn blossom from a small city-state to a vigorous power spreading outward from the capital in Lamm. As the Sultanate expanded east along the Copper Coast, it brought the teachings of Orthopraxy in its wake, replacing the primitive religion of Delisgar with enlightened right-doing. 

Though the Sultanate’s borders have been static for a generation, the ascension of a new sultan has seen a groundswell of popular support for further expansion. The seas to the north and the cities of the south hold great riches, if only they could be shown the wisdom of Orthopraxy. Where the Sultan’s soldiers march, all must give way.

## Identity

Rulership has always come easily to you. While lesser men debate and complain, you act decisively and win. Your honored father, peace be upon him, prepared you well for the throne, and you do your best to honor his memory. When the viziers complained that the treasury was running dry, you sent ships to sack the Diamond Isle and loot its mines. When the Delisgrene ambassador insulted you to your face, you ordered him beheaded. Simple solutions that your inferiors seem unable to see.

Now your eyes turn south, toward the city of Bemm. They speak Dinn there; why shouldn’t they welcome your rule? You’ve always wanted to command an army. Doubtless you’ll excel in that, as you do in all things.

- Age: 22
- Faith: Orthoprax
- Epithet: The Sailor

## Objectives

*As determined by yourself and the advice of your viziers.*

1.	Conquer the city of Bemm.
2.	Destroy the Allakian pirates.
3.	Open a trade route to the south.
